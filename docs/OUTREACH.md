## Share copy

### For AI engineers / technical communities

> LittleCures is an open-source project to accelerate cures for children with cancer. We are building open tools for pediatric trial matching, a target and IP registry, federated learning across hospitals, and global access advocacy.
>
> If you have ML, engineering, data, design, or clinical expertise, we need you. The repo is public, the roadmap is clear, and the work is patient-first.
>
> Repo: https://github.com/AEVentures/littlecures
> Site: https://littlecures.org/

### For rare disease and cancer advocates

> LittleCures is a public, open effort to speed up cures for the deadliest and most neglected pediatric cancers. We believe a child’s chance of survival should not depend on market size.
>
> We need engineers, clinicians, researchers, designers, and advocates. If you know someone who could help, please share.
>
> Repo: https://github.com/AEVentures/littlecures

### For clinical / research networks

> LittleCures is building shared, privacy-preserving infrastructure for pediatric oncology: open trial matching, target and IP landscape mapping, and federated learning.
>
> We are looking for clinical advisors, oncology researchers, bioinformaticians, and data scientists to keep the tools accurate and useful.
>
> Read the white paper: https://github.com/AEVentures/littlecures/blob/main/docs/WHITEPAPER.md

## What to ask people to do

1. Read the white paper and mission.
2. Look at the roadmap and open issues.
3. Open an issue or discussion if they want to help.
4. Pick a `good first issue` when one is created.
