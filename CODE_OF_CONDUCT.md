# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors, maintainers, and community members, pledge to make
participation in LittleCures a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for children and families affected by cancer
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation in any form
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub
repository, issue trackers, pull requests, documentation, and public
communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the maintainers at the project issue tracker. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned
with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
when appropriate.
